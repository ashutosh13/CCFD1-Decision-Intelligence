2. Personnel shows $0 for 2011-2018

The contracted series is the comparison. Personnel and contracted cost swap places in 2019-2020:

YearPersonnelContracted and professionalSum
2018$0$677,458$677,458
2019$1,300$727,980$729,280
2020$565,791$57,217$623,008
2021$648,290$57,195$705,485
  • Contracted cost sat at roughly $620-730K a year for 2011-2019, then fell about 92% in 2020, the same year personnel appeared. The combined total is similar either side of the change (about $680K in 2018, $623K in 2020).
  • Contracted cost was about 86-87% of operating spending in 2011 and 2018. That fits the idea that staffing was bought as a contracted service before the District hired its own staff.
  • So the zeros most likely mean "not on the District payroll yet", not "no staffing cost". This is an inference. The dashboard note already says the district moved from contracted service to its own staff in 2020.
  • The $57-65K left in contracted cost after 2020 is probably real professional services (legal, accounting, and so on). The series label is "contracted and professional services", so it is not purely staffing.
To confirm in the AFRs: the 2011-2019 contracted line items (who the contract was with), the 2019 partial-year payroll, and what the "Employee Benefits" line covers (the dashboard note says this is still being confirmed).
