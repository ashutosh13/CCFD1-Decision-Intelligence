Scenario 4: Compensation changes

Change base salary, the PERF 77 employer rate and the health premium per employee. Only base (first-class) salary is pensionable: a raise costs the salary plus the PERF share on top, while overtime, allowances and health premiums carry no pension cost. Per the partner, overtime is never zero: Scenario 4 assumes each firefighter works about the same overtime as last year (164 hrs), costed at the scenario salary.
